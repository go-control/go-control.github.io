What it will cover

Control, validated against the reference tools.

Every item starts from the roadmap's bibliography, is validated against the tool that is the reference in its domain, and lands with tests at 100% coverage and a performance table: no slower than the reference on one core.

⇄

LTI models

Transfer-function, zero-pole-gain, state-space and frequency-response models, with series, parallel and feedback, poles and zeros.

📈

Responses and margins

Time and frequency responses, and stability margins.

⚙️

Design

Riccati and Lyapunov equations, LQR and LQG, Kalman gain, pole placement and discretisation.

∂

DAE solver

An IDA-type variable-order BDF solver with consistent initial conditions and events.

🧩

Acausal modelling

MapleSim-style equation assembly with Pantelides index reduction, after the DAE solver and go-symbolic (wave 4).

🔗

Needs linear algebra

Real Schur form with reordering, QZ and expm, which go-ndarray's linalg does not have yet.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

On every architecture the fleet's CI tests. GPL oracles such as Octave's control package are compared against, never ported.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

Every repository of the organization.
